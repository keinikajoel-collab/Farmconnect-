# Buy straight from the farmer.

Farm Connect links Ugandan farmers with shops, restaurants and families. Farmers set their own prices. Buyers get fresh produce without extra middlemen.

## Fresh from the farm

Prices are per unit, in Uganda shillings. Contact the farmer to agree quantity and pickup.

## How Farm Connect works

Three steps from field to table.

1. ### Farmers list their harvest

   Add what you grow, how much you have and your price.
2. ### Buyers browse and choose

   Filter by produce and district to find the closest supply.
3. ### Agree and collect

   Call or message the farmer, agree the details, then arrange pickup or delivery.
